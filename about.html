❤️ About LifeLink

LifeLink is a Blood Donation Management System developed to connect blood donors with people in need. The platform helps users quickly search for available blood donors and allows administrators to manage donor records efficiently.

🎯 Our Mission

To save lives by making blood donation simple, fast, and accessible through technology.
